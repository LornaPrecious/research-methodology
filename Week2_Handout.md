# Week 2: team workshop and submission

Work in a team of **2–4 students**. Use the 20-minute workshop to choose a working topic and start finding relevant papers. You can complete the submission after class. Your topic can evolve as you read.

## One team submission

- **Paper milestone: 5 points.** Include team members, a working topic, the search strategy and an initial bibliography with short relevance notes.
- **Contribution report: 1 point.** Start a shared log with an entry for every member, evidence of their work and their next responsibility.

These are parts of the existing course grades. The examples below are formats for the same submission, not additional assignments. Submit by the end of Week 2, following the exact deadline and submission location announced in class.
